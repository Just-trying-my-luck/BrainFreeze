# CHARACTER

## PIP PICKETT

### PROFILE

Full Name: Horace Phillip Pickett
Nickname: Pip
Date of Birth: 1898

### APPEARANCE

Pip is a frail and timid child with a waif-like appearance.

Hair: Blonde
Eyes: Piercing blue
Complexion: Pale

### PERSONALITY

#### TIMID AND ANXIOUS

Pip is extremely timid and anxious, primarily due to the
abusive nature of his father, William Pickett. He lives in
constant fear and strives to avoid drawing attention to himself.

#### SELECTIVE MUTISM

Pip suffers from selective mutism. He is only able to whisper
short sentences to his most trusted older siblings, Tommy and Hannah.

#### OBEDIENT

He meticulously follows rules to avoid angering his father
and to evade physical punishment.

### EMPLOYMENT

#### CHIMNEY SWEEP

Due to his tiny frame, Pip works as a chimney sweep.

#### FUNERAL MUTE

Pip is occasionally asked to serve as a funeral mute because
of his somber demeanor and natural quietness.
